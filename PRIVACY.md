# CodeShelf Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

CodeShelf is a Chrome extension that saves your own **Accepted** LeetCode solutions so you can find them again. This policy explains what it stores and where. CodeShelf is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LeetCode.

## What CodeShelf collects

When a submission you make on leetcode.com is judged **Accepted**, CodeShelf saves:

- the code you submitted and its language
- the problem's title, number, slug and URL
- the submission ID, runtime, memory and their percentiles
- when it was submitted and saved

To do this, CodeShelf reads the responses LeetCode's own page receives for your submission (the submit request and its result) on leetcode.com. It doesn't send any extra requests to LeetCode, and it doesn't read anything on other websites.

If you sign in, CodeShelf also stores your **email address** and a **sign-in session** (access and refresh tokens). Your password is never stored by the extension. With Google sign-in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with our sign-in provider; CodeShelf uses only the email address.

CodeShelf doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Where it's stored

- **On your device:** saved solutions and your session are kept in the extension's own browser storage.
- **In the cloud (only if you sign in):** your solutions are backed up to a database hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com), which also handles sign-in. Each account can only read, add and delete its own solutions.

## How it's used

Only to show your saved solutions back to you on any device where you sign in. Your data isn't sold, rented or shared with anyone, and isn't used for anything else.

## Your choices

- **Use it without an account:** solutions then stay on your device only.
- **Export:** the extension's popup has **Export JSON** to download everything you've saved.
- **Delete cloud data:** the popup's **Delete my cloud data** removes all your solutions from the cloud.
- **Delete local data:** removing the extension deletes everything it stored on that device.
- **Delete your account:** email the address below and your account and all its data will be deleted.
